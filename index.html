About

I like problems that don't have an obvious answer yet.

The kind where you have to poke at them from three different angles before something clicks. That's basically the thread connecting everything I do.

Curious by default

If something is complicated, my instinct is to take it apart and see how it works.

Friendly, not scary

I genuinely enjoy explaining things and being explained to. Ask me anything.

Precision + play

Rigorous where it counts, silly where it doesn't. Usually in the same sentence.

Showing the work

Whether it's math or code, the reasoning matters as much as the result.

How I got here

I started coding because I wanted to make a calculator that showed its work — that turned into a habit of building small tools to answer whatever question was stuck in my head. Somewhere along the way I fell in love with the process: the parsing, the debugging, the moment the thing finally runs.

What I'm working on now

Mostly tools at the intersection of code and the other things I care about — math education, clinical support, aviation, and (yes) cheese.
